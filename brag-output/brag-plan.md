# Brag Plan: Aegis Protocol

## What is this app?
Aegis Protocol is an enterprise-grade autonomous multi-agent intelligence platform that detects, deconstructs, and neutralizes viral misinformation, stock manipulation, and digital smear campaigns in real time.

## The angle
"The truth travels slow. Lies travel viral. Aegis deploys an autonomous fleet of 7 AI sentinels to intercept digital threats before they break markets, reputations, and minds."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast cyber radar alert flashes in neon emerald and cyan: "THREAT DETECTED: Coordinated Narrative Attack In Progress." Glitch sound effect hits as real-time sentiment anomalies spike across Twitter, Reddit, and YouTube.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Scene 2 (4-9s) — The 7-Agent Autonomous Fleet:**
  Instant deployment grid reveals Scout (Financial Short-Attack Sentinel), Trend Pulse (Virality & Hawkes Contagion), BrandShield (Counterfeit & Smear Defense), and Personal Watch (Deepfake & VIP Defamation).
- **Scene 3 (9-14s) — Truth Dossier & Byzantine Consensus:**
  Live claim ingestion runs a multi-agent debate: cross-examining Reddit, Twitter/X, and News wires in real time to produce an undeniable cryptographic Truth Dossier with confidence scoring.
- **Scene 4 (14-17s) — Counter-Adversarial Strike:**
  Automated deployment of counter-narrative evidence packages and instant crisis mitigation packages.

## Outro / punchline (17-20s)
"Don't get blind-sided. Verify at machine speed."
Bold Aegis crest lockup with glowing shield icon, URL `aegis-protocol.ai`, and Swagger API badge.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Live claim input & high-velocity social signal ingestion.
2. Action: Parallel multi-agent analysis + Byzantine consensus weighting.
3. Result: Truth Dossier generated with refuting evidence matrix & threat neutralization package.
