## 1. Classification Taxonomy

To ensure safety and scientific reproducibility, files and directories evaluated during the Phase 7.2 workspace audit are categorized under one of six strict lifecycle states:

1. **`[PROTECTED DATA]`**: Immutable benchmark datasets, historical evidence dossiers, and empirical audit records. **Never delete, overwrite, or archive.**
2. **`[HISTORICAL ARTIFACT]`**: Development records, milestone audits, or architectural proposals from previous phases that preserve decision lineage. Kept in place or indexed under `docs/archive/`.
3. **`[NEEDS COMPATIBILITY WRAPPER]`**: Components that can be reorganized or moved only if a transparent, backwards-compatible entrypoint remains at the original path.
4. **`[REQUIRES OWNER APPROVAL]`**: External files, career rubrics, or obsolete assets whose removal or relocation requires explicit repository owner authorization.
5. **`[SAFE TO REORGANIZE]`**: Internal documentation or tooling that can be consolidated without impacting external consumers.
6. **`[UNVERIFIED DEPENDENCY]`**: Assets whose callers or downstream usages remain undetermined. Left untouched until full usage trace is established.
