Financial Markets & Filings
Scout Agent
Available • On Demand

Discovers emerging financial discussions, analyzes unusual price volatility, and cross-references market rumors against statutory regulatory filings.

Best for: Tracking market rumors, abnormal trading volatility, and corroborating corporate announcements with SEC filings.
Sources & Inputs
▲ Financial Market Quotes ◆ SEC EDGAR Filings ■ Corporate Press Wires ● Financial Forums
Technical details
Measures volume and return deviations as standard deviations (Z-scores) from recent baselines. Cross-checks entity mentions against verified SEC CIK registries and corporate 8-K/10-Q filing indexes.

Emerging Topics & Signals
Trending Agent
Available • On Demand

Tracks discussion velocity and volume acceleration across public channels to surface emerging narratives before they consolidate.

Best for: Early observation of rapidly spreading topics, discussion velocity comparisons, and multi-channel rumor discovery.

▲ Public News RSS Syndication ◆ Public Forum Streams ■ Multi-Channel Feeds ● Historical Volume Baselines

Brand & Entity Monitoring
BrandShield Agent
Available • On Demand

Screens marketplace product listings, customer reviews, and domain registrations for duplicate content and potential impersonation patterns.

Best for: Brand impersonation screening, duplicate review detection, and lookalike domain evaluation.
Sources & Inputs
▲ Public Search Feeds ◆ Marketplace Catalogs ■ Consumer Review Outlets ● Domain Registries

Applies text similarity heuristics (n-gram overlap, burst timing) across review corpora and evaluates edit-distance strings for domain typosquatting detection. Distinguishes observed similarity from proven coordinated campaigns.
