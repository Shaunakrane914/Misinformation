// Epistemic Standards

Three distinct information layers

Aegis strictly separates what was directly retrieved from what was inferred, and segregates mathematical simulations from empirical facts.

01 • OBSERVED EVIDENCE

What the sources state

Direct excerpts from verified wire registries, scientific journals, corporate filings, and archived publications. Never altered or hallucinated.

02 • INFERRED REASONING

What the evidence implies

Contradiction detection, chronological discrepancy mapping, and independent lineage analysis comparing distinct publisher clusters.

03 • SIMULATED MODELS

Controlled sandbox

Theoretical Hawkes diffusion processes and power-law distribution models. Explicitly marked as research instruments, not observed real-world truth.
